# NOTLAC, Inc. - Corporate Design Handbook
*Internal Document · 2026*
*Aligning NIST AI RMF Compliance with Operational Reality.*

---

## 1. The Brand Mark (Logo)
The Notlac, Inc. primary logo is the cornerstone of our visual identity. It balances the technological aspect of AI consulting with the steadfast reliability required by law enforcement and public safety agencies.

* **The Icon:** A precise Neural Cyan square with slightly rounded corners (border-radius: 6px). The inset 'N' is bold and rendered in Command Navy. It represents a digital node or an application icon, speaking directly to our software integrations (Flex, Command Central).
* **The Wordmark:** "NOTLAC" is set in Extrabold, tracking tight. "Inc." is set in a lighter weight and colored Tactical Slate to create visual hierarchy.
* **Clear Space:** The logo must always have a minimum padding equal to the height of the cyan icon on all sides.

---

## 2. Official Color Palette
Our color palette is strictly constrained to three core colors. This restraint projects authority and mirrors the interfaces of mission-critical dispatch and command center environments.

* **COMMAND NAVY (`#0B1E36`)**
  * *Usage:* Primary color. Deep backgrounds, hero sections, and heavy typography. 
* **NEURAL CYAN (`#00D4FF`)**
  * *Usage:* Accent color. Exclusively for primary buttons, active links, and digital highlights.
* **TACTICAL SLATE (`#475569`)**
  * *Usage:* Neutral color. Borders, secondary text, and inactive UI elements.

---

## 3. Typography Hierarchy
We utilize the **Inter** (or Noto Sans) font family for its exceptional legibility on digital screens. The hierarchy relies on extreme weight contrast rather than multiple font families.

* **Heading 1 (H1) - Extrabold:** Used for main page titles, Hero sections, and primary statements. Color: Command Navy.
* **Heading 2 (H2) - Bold:** Used for section breaks (e.g., "Comprehensive Consulting Services"). Color: Command Navy or Tactical Slate.
* **Body Copy (p) - Regular:** Used for all standard paragraph text. Maintain a relaxed line-height (1.5 - 1.6) for readability. Color: Dark Gray (`#374151`).

---

## 4. User Interface (UI) Architecture
Our web architecture (Next.js + Tailwind) relies on sharp, high-contrast UI elements. Buttons should look clickable, tactical, and definitive.

* **Primary Call to Action (CTA):**
  * *Style:* Solid Neural Cyan background with Command Navy bold text. Subtle cyan drop shadow.
  * *Use Case:* "Book a Consultation", "Schedule Audit".
* **Secondary Action Button:**
  * *Style:* Transparent background with a Tactical Slate border and text.
  * *Use Case:* "Explore Our Services", "Client Login".
* **Border Radius:** `rounded-md` (6px) on all UI cards and buttons.

---

## 5. Brand Voice & Positioning
Notlac, Inc. is not a generic tech startup. We are law enforcement professionals who understand enterprise software. Our copy must always reflect this pedigree.

* **The "Reality Gap":** We clearly articulate the difference between theoretical AI policy and actual frontline execution.
* **Direct Experience:** We explicitly leverage our 7 years of criminal justice experience (Investigator, Detention Officer, 911 Dispatcher) to build instant trust with agency leadership.
* **Software Fluency:** We confidently reference our 4 years of mission-critical software onboarding, naming the environments our clients use daily: *Motorola Solutions, Flex, Mobile Video, Command Central, and Spillman.*
